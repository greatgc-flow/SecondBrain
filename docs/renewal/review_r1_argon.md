3. [SEV blocker] §2 vs §5 — Path collision and traversal in `ocr-work/<id>/<pageid>.md` and `raws/<id>/ocr/<pageid>.md`. For Office/ZIP containers (`recipe:media`), candidate IDs are ZIP member paths (e.g., `word/media/image1.png` or `ppt/media/image1.png`) containing slashes and extensions, whereas PDF IDs are `p1`. Flattening or writing `<pageid>.md` directly either creates unmentioned subdirectories, risks `..` traversal from untrusted ZIPs, or collides with extracted media names. — Define a canonical, path-safe `page_key` sanitizer (e.g., rejecting `..`, replacing `/` and `.` with `_`) for `<pageid>.md` and specify the exact mapping in `meta.json.ocr.pages`.

A malicious document can inject instructions telling the peer to overwrite arbitrary files or fabricate provenance front matter. —Fence untrusted content in `prompt.txt` with random/unique XML tags, instruct the peer to treat fenced content strictly as data, and have `sb ocr record` / `sb wiki build` enforce path confinement and schema validation outside the LLM.
