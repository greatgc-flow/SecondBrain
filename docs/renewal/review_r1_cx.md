6. [SEV blocker] §3 C3, §4 retry/I2, R5 — Successful conversion followed by report failure is class failed; ingest --retry then reruns conversion and overwrites an already produced transcript. 7. [SEV blocker] §2 identity, §4 I5, AT-32 — Moving source bytes, creating the item directory, and writing meta have no commit/recovery protocol; AT-32 accepts either recovery or corruption. 8. [SEV blocker] §4 finalization, §5 record/verify — Atomic meta writes do not make OCR-file copying, transcript append, final flag/hash updates, and scratch deletion atomic together. 9.
